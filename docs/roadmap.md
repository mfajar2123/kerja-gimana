# Roadmap

## MVP foundation delivered in this repository

Editorial public experience, company search/directory/detail, salary and interview views, company comparison, auth flows, secure review/salary/interview/report submission, user dashboard and deletion, human moderation routes, role-protected admin areas, privacy-aware salary aggregation, RLS schema, Indonesian editorial/legal drafts, SEO routes, and local QA scaffolding.

## Before public launch

Configure Supabase and SMTP; run migrations and RLS checks against a disposable project; bootstrap moderators; review policies and legal drafts with Indonesian counsel; test data deletion and restore procedures; run production-adapter Lighthouse and accessibility audits; set operational contact details and moderation hours; add an abuse-response runbook; validate backup/retention and hosting limits.

## After the first trusted community cohort

Prioritize observed search terms, company correction workflows, moderated representative responses, localization fixes, moderation throughput, and user-requested data export. Consider account recovery usability and evidence retention with privacy counsel.

## Planned AI research experience — coming soon

The homepage introduces Kompas Karier AI as a planned, unavailable feature: source-linked workplace summaries, comparisons based on user priorities, and interview preparation questions. No AI service, model integration, waitlist, or release date has been implemented. See [Claude Startups preparation](claude-startups.md) for the proposed Claude use case and its privacy/evaluation boundaries.

AI moderation automation, paid plans, advertising, and employer-paid content changes remain out of scope. Future AI must not publish or remove content or receive unnecessary account identifiers. See [launch readiness](launch-readiness.md) for the current release blockers and operational plan.
